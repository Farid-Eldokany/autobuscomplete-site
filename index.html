🚌 Autobus Complete

The word game where everyone races to fill six categories with words starting with the same letter — then calls “Autobus Complete!”

Support

Something not working, or an idea for the game? Email us at autobuscompleteservices@gmail.com and we'll get back to you.

Quick help

  • How to play: open the app and tap How to play on the menu.
  • Playing with friends: Multiplayer → Create room, then share the 5-letter room code. Friends tap Join and type it in.
  • Sound and music: tap the ⚙️ in the top-right corner of any screen.
  • “Can't reach the game”: check your internet connection; the app reconnects by itself.
  • “Time for an update”: install the latest version from the App Store or Google Play.
