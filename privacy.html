Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 October 2026

Autobus Rush: Word Race is a word game for all ages. It has no accounts, no sign-up and no chat. This page explains the little information the game uses, and why.

What the game uses

  • The name you choose for playing (it doesn't have to be your real name). Other players in your game see it. Names are checked for family-friendly wording.
  • A random player ID created on your device, so you can rejoin your game after a disconnect. It isn't linked to who you are.
  • Your game answers (words like "a country starting with B"). They're shown to the other players in your game and scored by our AI referee.
  • Your settings and your word book (sound, music, an optional promo code, and the list of words you've played) are stored only on your device and never sent to us.
  • Error reports: if the app crashes, it sends us a technical report (the error, the app version, the type of device) so we can fix it. Reports contain no personal information.

How long we keep it

  • Games (names, answers, scores) are deleted automatically 24 hours after the last activity in the room.
  • Error reports are deleted after 7 days.
  • Anything stored on your device is removed when you delete the app.

Who else processes it

  • Amazon Web Services and MongoDB Atlas run our game servers and database.
  • OpenRouter forwards the answers of each round (just the words, the round's letter and language, with no names or IDs) to an AI model that checks them. Answers flagged as not family friendly are filtered out before that.
  • Google AdMob shows occasional ads. Ads are set up for a general audience including children: only content rated for all ages, no personalised ads, and Google's consent form where the law requires it. Google may use device information to show and measure these ads; see how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

We don't sell or share your information with anyone else, and we don't use it for anything except running the game.

Children

The game is designed to be safe for all ages: no accounts, no personal details asked, no chat between players, family-friendly filters on names and answers, and non-personalised, all-ages ads.

Your choices

  • You can play under any name and change it any time.
  • Deleting the app removes everything stored on your device. Game data on our servers disappears within 24 hours anyway.
  • Questions or requests: autobuscompleteservices@gmail.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
